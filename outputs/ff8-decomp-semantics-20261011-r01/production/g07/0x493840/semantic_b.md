The natural ABI representation has three caller-owned stack arguments; it does not establish a particular original C signed type for `s` or a full-width semantic type for `a`.

## Complete effect sequence

If it is nonzero, low BYTE `a & 0x35` is zero, and `b & 0x14000` is zero, set or clear slot flag bit `0x400` according to `b & 9`. This does **not** clear incoming EBX's high bit, so subsequent tests still use the original incoming high bit.
2. If low BYTE `a & 4` or `b & 9` is nonzero, call `0x484600` with slot on the stack. If its **full EAX equals zero** and incoming `b & 0x80000000` is zero, perform two separate DWORD read/modify/stores clearing slot flag `0x08` and then `0x04`, and call `0x485030` with slot. A nonzero helper result skips these changes; incoming high bit also bypasses later change-detection handling.
3. When incoming high bit is zero, compare low BYTE `a` against low BYTE `[0x1D28E30+2*s]` for changed bit `0x20`. If unchanged, test changed bits `0x02004000` between incoming `b` and DWORD `[0x1D28E38+4*s]`. In particular `0x80000000` is tested as a bit, not used to establish signed negativity. No MOVSX or x87/SIMD operation occurs.
